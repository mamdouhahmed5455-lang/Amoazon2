STUDY AREA RATIONALE

Why Rondônia?

Rondônia is the current prototype study area.

The pilot focuses on a highly fragmented, road-influenced landscape, providing a useful setting for testing spatial risk prioritization.

2004 Peak: 3,858 km²
2021 Rebound: 1,673 km²

2025 Clearing: 229 km²

Rondônia is selected for its dense road access (BR-364 fishbone) and sharp protected enclaves, not as an absolute highest-risk claim.
Scientific Methodology & System Separation: PRODES measures annual consolidated primary forest clear-cutting via cloud-free Landsat/Sentinel/CBERS mosaics (≥6.25 ha). It should not be interpreted as a real-time alert stream. DETER provides rapid daily/weekly radiometric alerts (≥3 ha) for operational field enforcement (IBAMA), subject to persistent cloud cover. PRODES and DETER figures represent distinct monitoring methodologies and cannot be mathematically conflated.
