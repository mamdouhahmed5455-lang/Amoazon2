# Model Governance & Responsible AI Framework

> **Governance Standard**: OECD AI Principles & High-Level Expert Group on AI (HLEG-AI)  
> **Operational Domain**: Environmental Intelligence & Spatial Prioritization

---

## 1. Ethical Principles & Purpose

The GeoAI Deforestation Risk Platform is engineered under a foundational ethical commitment: **technology must empower human environmental defenders, protect indigenous sovereignty, and preserve planetary biosphere stability without overstepping legal boundaries or automating coercive state force.**

---

## 2. Core Governance Directives

### Directive 1: Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)
- **Zero Autonomous Enforcement**: The system is strictly prohibited from autonomously issuing legal notices, property embargoes, administrative fines, or law enforcement dispatch orders.
- **Verification Protocol**: Every high-priority risk flag generated by the spatial scoring pipeline must undergo independent manual verification by a certified remote-sensing analyst or ground patrol commander before physical resources are mobilized.
- **Decision-Support Boundary**: The algorithm advises *where to look*; human domain experts decide *whether and how to act*.

### Directive 2: Data Drift & Concept Drift Telemetry
In future operational deployments (Stages 3 & 4), the model must be monitored for performance degradation caused by shifting frontier dynamics:
- **Covariate (Data) Drift**: Monitoring changes in feature distributions (e.g., changes in road network topology, population redistribution, or sensor calibration shifts).
- **Concept Drift**: Shifts in the underlying relationship between features and deforestation (e.g., changes in timber market prices, land titling legislation, or new clandestine clearing techniques that avoid road proximity).
- **Audit Trigger**: If annual out-of-time AUC drops below **0.78** or F1 drops below **0.75**, an automated model retraining flag is triggered.

### Directive 3: Versioning, Lineage & Rollback
- **Semantic Model Versioning**: Every deployed model must be versioned (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`) alongside its corresponding training data hash and feature pipeline snapshot.
- **Model Lineage**: A complete manifest linking input satellite tiles, training code commits, hyperparameter configurations, and evaluation reports must be preserved in a secure registry.
- **Rollback Protocol**: If an updated model exhibits localized over-prediction or false-alarm spikes in a specific municipal district, operators must be able to roll back to the previously validated model version within $<1\text{ hour}$.

### Directive 4: Geographic Expansion Safeguards
- **Prohibition of Uncalibrated Generalization**: A model trained in the road-influenced biome of Rondônia must **never** be deployed operationally across other Amazonian states (such as Pará, Mato Grosso, or Amazonas) without prior regional validation and recalibration.
- **Ecosystem Heterogeneity**: Different Amazonian sub-regions possess vastly different clearing patterns (e.g., large industrial soy plots in Mato Grosso vs. smallholder slash-and-burn in Acre). Models must be independently evaluated on local data.

### Directive 5: Indigenous & Traditional Community Safeguards
- **Boundary Sensitivity**: Predictions intersecting demarcated Indigenous Territories (*Terras Indígenas*) and Extractive Reserves (*Reservas Extrativistas*) must be prioritized for defensive monitoring while strictly protecting the privacy and territorial autonomy of indigenous communities.
- **Do No Harm**: Spatial vulnerability scores must not be repurposed by private entities to identify and exploit accessible forest frontiers.

## 3. Transparency & Accountability Matrix

| Operational Component | Governance Requirement | Responsible Entity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spatial Risk Scoring** | Algorithmic transparency via SHAP feature importance | Modeling Team |
| **High-Risk Flag Triage**| Secondary verification via high-resolution optical/SAR | Remote Sensing Analyst |
| **Enforcement Dispatch** | Ground truth inspection, legal warrants, and chain of custody | Sovereign Agency (IBAMA/ICMBio) |
| **Outcome Confirmation** | Independent retrospective audit using INPE PRODES census | External Oversight / Auditing |
| **Model Retraining** | Annual recalibration, drift auditing, and stakeholder review | MLOps Engineering Team |
