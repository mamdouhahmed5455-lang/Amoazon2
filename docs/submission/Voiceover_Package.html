Narrator Persona, Cadence & Voice Control
CREATOR PERSONA: RESEARCH-DRIVEN, CONFIDENT, DISCIPLINED
The voice should sound like the principal developer and researcher presenting their own authentic engineering work: calm, articulate, intellectually rigorous, and completely free of artificial commercial enthusiasm. Avoid sounding like a generic corporate voiceover artist or a hyped tech product trailer.
PACING & BREATH PROTOCOL 130–140 WPM
Measured Pace: Maintain a disciplined 135 words per minute. Do not rush technical terms or metric recitations.
Micro-Pauses (0.3s): Insert distinct mini-pauses after commas and before contrasting clauses (e.g., “...exploratory sensitivity model [pause] not live forecasting”).
Full Stop Breaths (0.6s): Take deliberate, silent breaths at sentence boundaries to prevent vocal fatigue or pitch rise.
EMPHASIS & INFLECTION MAP Scientific Precision
Primary Stress Words: Stress proactive risk-screening, relative risk surface, two distinct evidence tracks, and verification planning.
Down-Toned Non-Claims: Lower pitch and use a matter-of-fact cadence when stating boundaries: “not live forecasting”, “not to replace established systems”.
Zero Up-Talk: Avoid rising pitch at sentence endings (up-talk); end every factual statement on a firm, authoritative downward inflection.

Master Audio, Video & Subtitle Technical Specifications
AUDIO CAPTURE & MASTERING -16 LUFS
Microphone: Large-diaphragm condenser or broadcast dynamic mic placed 15–20 cm from mouth at 45° off-axis with pop filter.
Gain Staging: Peak levels between -12 dB and -6 dB. Low room noise floor (< -50 dB). Zero digital clipping.
Target Loudness: Mastered to -16 LUFS Integrated (±0.5 LUFS) with -1.0 dB True Peak ceiling.
Music Mix: Subtle acoustic/ambient bed mixed at -24 dB to -28 dB beneath voice-over. Zero percussion clash.
SCREEN RECORDING PROTOCOL 1080p 60FPS
Resolution & Framerate: 1920×1080 Full HD (or 3840×2160 4K) at 60 fps constant framerate (CFR).
Clean Browser Chrome: Fullscreen mode (F11); hide bookmark bar, extension icons, URL bar, and OS taskbar.
Cursor Discipline: Smooth cursor pathing; no jittery circles or erratic clicks. Use cursor highlighting only if subtle.
Map Motion: Smooth continuous camera rotations (Deck.gl damping active); avoid sudden zoom step jumps.
