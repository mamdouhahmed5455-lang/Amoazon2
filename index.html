PROBLEM CONTEXT

The Amazon rainforest is undergoing accelerated deforestation driven by road expansion, agricultural pressure, and frontier development.

These pressures generate fragmented spatial patterns that are difficult to detect early using manual interpretation alone.

Platform Goal: We developed a GeoAI platform that integrates satellite data, machine learning prediction, and spatial analysis to identify and prioritize likely future forest loss zones.

The proposed GeoAI framework integrates satellite data, XGBoost machine learning, spatial risk modeling, and interactive 3D visualization to estimate deforestation risk across the Amazon Basin.

Key Cycle: Satellite Data -> XGBoost Classification -> Risk Calculation -> Spatial Validation
Model Validation Workflow: Prediction -> Satellite validation -> Accuracy assessment

ANALYSIS & VALIDATION

The model achieved an AUC score of 0.82, indicating strong predictive discrimination between higher-risk and lower-risk cells. Spatial validation was performed by comparing predicted hotspots with observed loss proxies, active fire hotspots, and satellite-based validation indicators.

STUDY AREA: RONDONIA, BRAZIL

The study focuses on the Arc of Deforestation in Rondonia, Brazil. Approximately 150,000 spatial samples were analyzed to identify high-risk zones associated with agricultural expansion and illegal logging corridors.

The operational objective is early warning: prioritize inspections, reduce field search effort, and surface the most critical hotspots first.

DEFENSE QUESTIONS

1. Why did you choose the model?
The selected model is suitable for multi-factor spatial prediction, handles non-linear relationships effectively, and supports interpretable feature ranking for key deforestation drivers.

2. What does AUC = 0.82 mean?
It means the model has strong discriminative ability to separate higher-risk cells from lower-risk cells. A value of 0.82 indicates good predictive performance above random classification.

3. How was the model validated?
Validation was performed by comparing predicted high-risk locations with observed forest loss proxies, fire hotspots, and satellite-based spatial validation indicators.

4. What is the difference between heatmap and hotspot?
Heatmap shows the overall spatial intensity of predicted risk across the region, while hotspot mode isolates the most critical high-risk cells for direct intervention and prioritization.

STUDY AREA RATIONALE

Why Rondônia?

Rondônia is the current prototype study area.

The pilot focuses on a highly fragmented, road-influenced landscape, providing a useful setting for testing spatial risk prioritization.

2004 Peak: 3,858 km²
2021 Rebound: 1,673 km²
2024 Clearing: 360 km²
2025 Clearing: 229 km²
2024 → 2025 Trend: ↓ 36.39%
Rondônia is selected for its dense road access (BR-364 fishbone) and sharp protected enclaves, not as an absolute highest-risk claim.
Scientific Methodology & System Separation: PRODES measures annual consolidated primary forest clear-cutting via cloud-free Landsat/Sentinel/CBERS mosaics (≥6.25 ha). It should not be interpreted as a real-time alert stream. DETER provides rapid daily/weekly radiometric alerts (≥3 ha) for operational field enforcement (IBAMA), subject to persistent cloud cover. PRODES and DETER figures represent distinct monitoring methodologies and cannot be mathematically conflated.
